# AI Enterprise Features (TSA Legacy)

## 1. Smart Invoice Scanner (OCR via Gemini)
**UseCase:** Upload a photo or PDF of a purchase invoice from a supplier, and the Gemini AI will extract the Invoice Number, Date, Supplier Name, and create a list of all products (with Qty and Rate) automatically.
**Integration:** Added directly into the "New Purchase" page. 

## 2. Smart Cash-Flow Prediction (Future)
**UseCase:** Analyze past sales and purchases to predict next month’s cash flow.

## 3. Auto-Categorization & Mapping (Future)
**UseCase:** Using AI to guess product tax slabs and categories automatically based on the HSN code or item name.

## 4. Intelligent Restock Alerts (Future)
**UseCase:** AI learns the sale pattern and warns the admin, "You have 5 packets left, and usually, you sell 5 a week. Time to order."
